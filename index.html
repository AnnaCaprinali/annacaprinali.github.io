About Me

I am an Early Career Researcher working on social stratification, educational inequalities, gender, and sexual orientation.

My research combines demographic and sociological perspectives to investigate how inequalities emerge and develop throughout the life course.

Current Position

Thesis title:
Diverging trajectories? Educational stratification and life opportunities of LGB adolescents.

Research Interests

My research focuses on social stratification and demography, with particular attention to inequalities related to gender and sexual orientation.

Recent work examines how sexual orientation shapes educational careers, partnership transitions, and broader life trajectories.

My doctoral research explored the relationship between sexual orientation, educational attainment, and adult opportunities among LGBTQIA+ individuals.
