Research interests

My research is situated at the intersection of social stratification and demography, using an interdisciplinary approach to investigate potential inequalities resulting
from the interaction of gender and sexual orientation in various aspects of life.

My PhD project explored the extent to which sexual orientationis related to educational attainment, and how it contributes to shape adult opportunities for LGB(TQIA*) pupils.

More recently, my interests has been enlarged to social isolation and loneliness,
aspects of mental health and behaviour that are closely linked and outrcomes of social relations and wellbeing.
