02 / Research

Social inequalities, in different forms

My doctoral research investigated educational inequalities related to sexual orientation and how these intersect with opportunities across the life course.

More broadly, I am interested in how social structures, institutions, and relationships shape people's educational, partnership, and family trajectories.
